# \dv definition (vernacular)

*\key* \dv

This is the hardest of all fields to fill in, because it requires the researcher to explain, in the
vernacular, what the salient concepts are (i.e. the domain) that this "unit of meaning" captures
-- from a native speaker's perspective. Obviously not easy and something usually left to much
later. 

For the related definition fields, see:

\cf \de, \dn, and \dr

This field is also used for creating a monolingual dictionary. For related monolingual fields, see:

\cf \gv, \uv, \ov, and \ev
