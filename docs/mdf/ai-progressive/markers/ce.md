# \ce cross-reference (English gloss)

*\key* \ce

This gives the English gloss for the vernacular lexeme referenced by the preceding \cf field. 

For more information, see:

\cf \cf confer/cross-reference

Identical national and regional language gloss fields are:

\cf \cn and \cr
